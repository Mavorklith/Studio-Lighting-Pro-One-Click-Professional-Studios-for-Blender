# Assets and sharing

All five featured objects were downloaded from Poly Haven as CC0 assets. These models remain freely available from their original source; they are credited here and are not represented as exclusive Studio Lighting creations.

- [Marble Bust 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_bust_01)
- [Mid Century Lounge Chair](https://polyhaven.com/a/mid_century_lounge_chair)
- [Horse Statue 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/horse_statue_01)
- [Bull Head](https://polyhaven.com/a/bull_head)
- [Dining Table](https://polyhaven.com/a/dining_table)

[Poly Haven's asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) permits commercial rendering and redistribution of the CC0 model assets. The original website screenshots and website preview renders are not included; the supplied promotional images are newly rendered in Blender using Studio Lighting.

The background objects are original procedural geometry generated by `studio_lighting/sets.py`. No external foliage, HDRIs, textures, or branded objects are required by those backgrounds.

`Original-Music.wav` is an original synthesized instrumental created for this project, without third-party samples. Its composition and recording are supplied under CC0. `Narration.wav` was synthesized locally with the installed Microsoft Zira Desktop voice; it is synthetic narration, not a human performance or voice clone. No music subscription or online TTS service was used.
